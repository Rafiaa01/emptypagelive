About

Backend-first, AI-native.

I'm a Software Engineering graduate (BSSE, 3.79 GPA) and VC Talent Scholarship winner. I like the part of AI projects most people skip past: the API that has to keep responding correctly at 2am, the auth flow that has to survive a rate-limit spike, the database schema that has to still make sense six months in.

My work leans on FastAPI, Flask, Postgres, and Supabase, with LLMs (via Groq and Hugging Face) as a component of the system rather than the whole system. I write down what broke and what I'd do differently — that accounting is part of the deliverable, not an afterthought.
